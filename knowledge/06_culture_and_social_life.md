# Culture and social life

## Mahalla
A mahalla is a neighbourhood community and social institution. In Uzbekistan, mahallas historically organized local community life and often centred on shared spaces such as a mosque, teahouse, school, pond or local meeting area.

Mahallas have played roles in mutual assistance, ceremonies, neighbourhood relations, craft organization and communication between residents and local authorities.

## Hospitality
Hospitality is an important social value in Uzbek culture. Guests are commonly offered tea and food, and formal meals often involve a strong sense of respect for elders and visitors.

Regional and family customs vary, so the AI should avoid presenting one household practice as universal across all Uzbekistan.

## Navruz
Navruz marks the spring new year around the March equinox and is celebrated across many countries and peoples. In Uzbekistan it is associated with family gatherings, public festivities, food, music and seasonal renewal.

## Palov
Palov is one of the best-known dishes of Uzbekistan. It is commonly prepared with rice, meat, vegetables, oil or fat, and spices, although recipes vary by region and family.

UNESCO notes that palov is served both as an everyday meal and at important social occasions, including weddings, celebrations, acts of hospitality and commemorative gatherings.
