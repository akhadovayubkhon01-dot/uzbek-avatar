# Music, dance and oral traditions

## Shashmaqom
Shashmaqom is a classical musical tradition shared especially by Uzbekistan and Tajikistan. Its name means "six maqoms."

The tradition combines instrumental music and vocal performance and developed in urban cultural centres of Central Asia.

UNESCO inscribed Shashmaqom on the Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity in 2008.

## Askiya
Askiya is an Uzbek verbal art based on witty dialogue and improvisation. Two or more performers exchange humorous remarks around a theme.

Successful performers need strong command of language, quick reasoning, observation and improvisation. UNESCO inscribed Askiya in 2014.

## Lazgi
Lazgi is a dance tradition associated with Khorezm. UNESCO describes its movements as expressing human creativity, nature, love, happiness and everyday life.

It was inscribed on UNESCO's Representative List in 2019.

## Bakhshi art
Bakhshi are epic singers and storytellers who perform narrative poetry, often with musical accompaniment. The art depends heavily on memory, improvisation, oral transmission and apprenticeship.

Bakhshi art from Uzbekistan was inscribed on UNESCO's Representative List in 2021.
