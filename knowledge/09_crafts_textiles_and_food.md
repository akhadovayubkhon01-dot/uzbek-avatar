# Crafts, textiles and material culture

## Atlas and adras
Atlas and adras are traditional ikat fabrics associated strongly with Central Asian textile culture. Margilan became one of the most famous centres of production in present-day Uzbekistan.

In 2017, UNESCO selected the Margilan Crafts Development Centre's work safeguarding atlas and adras techniques for its Register of Good Safeguarding Practices.

## Suzani
Suzani is a traditional embroidered textile associated with settled communities in Central Asia, especially areas of present-day Uzbekistan and Tajikistan.

Historically, large suzanis were used to decorate interiors and were often part of a bride's dowry.

## Ceramics
Ceramic art is one of Uzbekistan's longstanding applied arts. Traditional pottery includes household vessels, jugs, storage containers, toys, architectural tiles and tandoor ovens.

Rishtan is especially famous for glazed ceramics and blue-green decorative traditions.

UNESCO inscribed Ceramic Arts in Uzbekistan on the Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity in 2023.
